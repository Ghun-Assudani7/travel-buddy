### Local Customs & Etiquette

1. **Embrace *Susegad*:** Goa operates on *Susegad*—a philosophy of relaxed, unhurried living. Service at traditional family-run eateries can be slow; sit back and enjoy the atmosphere.
2. **Dress Code for Religious Sites:** Cover your shoulders and knees when visiting churches (e.g., Basilica of Bom Jesus) or temples (e.g., Mangueshi Temple). Beachwear/swimwear is strictly for the beach; wearing it through town centers or shops is frowned upon.
3. **Respect Village Privacy:** When exploring neighborhoods like Fontainhas or local villages, avoid photographing residents inside their private verandahs or balconies without permission.
4. **Feni Etiquette:** Feni (cashew/coconut spirit) is a craft spirit to be savored. Enjoy it the authentic way: diluted with a citrus mixer (like Limca), a pinch of salt, and a split green chili floating in the glass.
5. **Siesta Hours:** Small shops and bakeries in villages often close between 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM.

### 4-Day Tailored Itinerary

#### **Day 1: Arrival, Heritage Forts & Riverside Fine Dining**
* **Morning:** Arrive at MOPA (GOX) or Dabolim (GOI) Airport. Check into your boutique hotel (e.g., *Ahilya by the Sea* in Nerul or *The Postcard Hotel* in Velha Goa).
* **Afternoon:** Visit the restored **Reis Magos Fort** in Verem for sweeping panoramic views of the Mandovi estuary without the crowds of Fort Aguada. Drive through the quiet village of **Saligao** to view the neo-Gothic Mae de Deus Church.
* **Late Afternoon:** Relax on the serene sands of **Ashvem Beach** or **Morjim Beach**.
* **Evening:** Enjoy a cocktail at *For The Record Vinyl Bar* in Panaji, followed by dinner at *Hosa* (Siolim), set in a restored 100-year-old Goan villa serving progressive South Indian cuisine.

#### **Day 2: Fontainhas Heritage Walk, Island E-Biking & Private Sunset Charter**
* **Morning:** Take an early morning walking tour through **Fontainhas** (Panaji’s Latin Quarter) to view the colorful Portuguese-style homes. Stop at **Confeitaria 31 De Janeiro** for warm, wood-fired *Bebinca* and *prawn rissóis*.
* **Afternoon:** Take a short car ferry to **Divar Island**. Take an e-bike tour (via BLive) through paddy fields, ancient churches, and quiet village lanes. Stop for an authentic local *thali* and clam curry at **Kokni Kanteen** back in Panaji.
* **Late Afternoon:** Charter a private pontoon boat or yacht along the Chapora River backwaters for a private sunset cruise with drinks.
* **Evening:** Fine dining in Assagao at **Bomras**, a critically acclaimed Burmese restaurant set in an enchanting open-air garden.

#### **Day 3: Deep South Goa Secrets, Mansions & Hidden Lagoons**
* **Morning:** Drive south to **Loutolim**. Tour the historic 400-year-old **Figueiredo Mansion** and pick up fresh chocolate eclairs from the hidden, home-based **Jila Bakery**.
* **Afternoon:** Continue south to **Cabo de Rama Fort**. Walk down the secluded path to **Cola Beach** to swim in its freshwater lagoon where it opens into the Arabian Sea.
* **Late Afternoon:** Unwind on **Galgibaga Beach**, a peaceful, undeveloped sanctuary known as an Olive Ridley turtle nesting site.
* **Evening:** Dine by the water at **Fisherman’s Wharf** (Cavelossim) or **The Southern Deck** (Benaulim) for fresh, local Goan seafood (Rava fried Kingfish and Pork Vindaloo).

#### **Day 4: Artisan Boutiques, Feni Tasting & Departure**
* **Morning:** Spend a quiet morning browsing high-end fashion and artisan boutiques in **Assagao** (e.g., *Bungalow 8*, *Buna*, and *Cheshire Cat Jewelry*).
* **Afternoon:** Experience a private, pre-booked **Cazulo Feni Tasting Session** in Cansaulim, learning about traditional cashew/coconut distillation paired with local tapas.
* **Late Afternoon:** Pick up premium Goan cashews, spices, and fresh *Poee* bread from the Panaji Local Market before transferring to the airport for your flight home.
