### Day-by-Day Itinerary

#### Day 1: Central Heritage, Latin Quarter, Backwater Cruise & Cliffside Sunset

* **Morning (8:30 AM – 12:30 PM): Fontainhas & Island Escape**
  * Check into your luxury resort (e.g., *Ahilya by the Sea* in Nerul or *W Goa* in Vagator).
  * Begin in **Panjim's Latin Quarter (Fontainhas)**. Walk past vibrant Portuguese heritage villas and visit the iconic **Immaculate Conception Church**.
  * Stop at **Confeitaria 31 De Janeiro**, one of Goa’s oldest bakeries, for a fresh *Pastel de Nata* (Portuguese custard tart).
  * Take a short car-ferry over to **Divar Island** to explore quiet Portuguese-era villages, paddy fields, and hilltop views away from city noise.

* **Lunch (1:00 PM – 2:30 PM): Modern Coastal Fine Dining**
  * Dine at **Hosa** in Siolim—housed in a restored 19th-century Portuguese villa serving elevated Goan and South Indian coastal dishes paired with artisanal botanical cocktails.

* **Afternoon (3:00 PM – 5:30 PM): Private Yacht Charter & Coastal Forts**
  * Board a private luxury yacht from **Britona Jetty** or **Siolim Jetty**. Sail down the quiet Chapora or Mandovi river backwaters, passing lush mangroves and historic chapels.
  * Optionally visit **Reis Magos Fort** for panoramic coastal viewpoints.

* **Evening & Night (6:00 PM onwards): Sunset & Culinary Art**
  * Watch the sunset over the red cliffs at **Ozran Beach** (Vagator).
  * **Dinner:** Reserve a table at **Slow Tide** (Anjuna) for sophisticated beachfront dining or **Thalassa** / **Titlie** for high-end cliffside views and Mediterranean-Goan fusion.
  * **Nightcap:** Drop by **Joseph Bar** in Panjim for a local artisanal Feni cocktail, or **For The Record Vinyl Bar** for high-fidelity audio and craft drinks.

---

#### Day 2: South Goa Mansions, World Heritage, Secret Coves & Gastronomy

* **Morning (8:30 AM – 12:30 PM): Heritage Mansions & Secret Coves**
  * Drive south to Old Goa to visit UNESCO World Heritage landmarks: **Basilica of Bom Jesus** and **Se Cathedral**.
  * Proceed to Chandor to tour the **Figueiredo House** (or **Menezes Braganza House**), grand 16th-century private estates guided by family descendants.
  * Take a brief stroll down to **Kakolem Beach (Tiger Beach)** or **Utorda Beach** for a secluded coastal break.

* **Lunch (1:00 PM – 2:30 PM): Elevated Culinary Experience**
  * **Cavatina by Avinash Martins** (Benaulim). Experience a multi-course tasting menu that redefines traditional Goan heritage recipes into modern culinary art.

* **Afternoon (3:00 PM – 5:00 PM): Organic Spice Farm & Secret Bakery**
  * Visit **Tanshikar’s Working Organic Spice Farm** (Netravali) for a private guided walk focused on permaculture, vanilla, and rare spices.
  * On the return drive, stop at **Jila Bakery** in Loutolim—a hidden family-run bakery legendary for making India's crispest chocolate eclairs and *Geneva pastries*.

* **Evening & Night (6:30 PM onwards): Riverside Sundowner & Grand Dinner**
  * Catch the sunset along the quiet banks of the Sal River or at **Cola Beach** lagoon.
  * **Dinner:** **Riverside** at *The Leela* (Cavelossim) for fine-dining Italian along the river, or head to Assagao to dine at **Jamun** or **Gunpowder** in a romantic garden courtyard setting.

### Top Places to Visit (Summary)

1. **Fontainhas (Panjim):** Latin Quarter famous for colourful Portuguese architecture and historic bakeries.
2. **Divar Island:** Peaceful river island showcasing traditional village life (*Susegad*).
3. **Basilica of Bom Jesus & Se Cathedral (Old Goa):** Historic 16th-century UNESCO World Heritage churches.
4. **Figueiredo House (Chandor):** A museum-grade living Portuguese mansion with private antique collections.
5. **Kakolem / Utorda Beaches:** Pristine, quiet stretches of sand away from commercialized beach shacks.

### Food & Drink Recommendations

* **Must-Try Dishes:** Goan Fish Curry Rice, Chicken/Prawn Cafreal (vibrant green coriander/toddy vinegar marinade), Pork Vindaloo, Crab Xacuti, Choris Pao (spicy Goan smoked pork sausage in local sourdough bread), and Bebinca (layered Goan dessert).
* **Fine Dining Spots:** *Cavatina* (Benaulim), *Hosa* (Siolim), *Slow Tide* (Anjuna), *Mum’s Kitchen* (Panjim), *Riverside* at The Leela (Cavelossim).
* **Craft Drinks:** Cashew or Coconut Feni (try a local "GSL": Feni with Limca, salt, and green chili) and local Goan craft gins (*Stranger & Sons*, *Tamras*).

### Local Customs & Etiquette

* **Embrace Susegad:** Pace your expectations. *Susegad* is the Goan concept of a relaxed, quiet lifestyle. Many non-tourist shops close between 1:30 PM and 4:00 PM for afternoon rest.
* **Dress Code:** Beachwear is suitable for the coast, but cover shoulders and knees when entering places of worship (churches, temples) and inland villages. Remove shoes where indicated.
* **Environmental Respect:** Do not drive vehicles on beaches (it is illegal and damages turtle nesting grounds). Pay attention to beach flag indicators before swimming.
