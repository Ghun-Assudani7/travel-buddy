### Weather & Seasonal Guide

* **Spring (May–June):** 12°C to 20°C. Mild weather, blooming parks. Great for walking.
* **Summer (July–August):** 18°C to 26°C. Long daylight (until 9:30 PM), warm, packed crowds.
* **Autumn (September–October):** 10°C to 17°C. Crisp air, colorful foliage, light rain showers.
* **Winter (November–February):** 2°C to 9°C. Cold, early sunset (~4:00 PM), wet. Beautiful Christmas lights in Dec.

### Local Etiquette & Rules

1. **Escalators:** Stand on the **RIGHT**, walk on the **LEFT**. Blocking the left side on Tube escalators will cause immediate frustration from locals.
2. **The Pub Protocol:** Do not wait to be seated. Walk to the bar, order food/drinks, give your table number, and pay immediately. Ask for free "tap water."
3. **Cashless City:** Cash is almost obsolete. Tap your zero-forex contactless credit/debit card or phone (Apple/Google Pay) directly at transit gates and shops.
4. **Tipping:** A 12.5% discretionary service charge is automatically added to sit-down restaurant bills. Extra tipping is not required, nor is tipping at pub bars.
5. **Bus Travel:** Buses are flat-rate (£1.75). The "Hopper Fare" lets you take unlimited bus transfers within 1 hour for a single £1.75 fee.
6. **Queueing:** Always join the back of a line politely. Never cut in line.

### 10-Day Complete Itinerary

#### Day 1: Westminster Icons & Secret Ruin Gardens
* **Morning:** Arrive, check in to your stay, grab an Oyster card or use contactless transit.
* **Afternoon:** Stroll past **Big Ben**, **Westminster Abbey** (exterior), and the **Palace of Westminster**. Walk to **St Dunstan in the East**, a stunning medieval church destroyed in WWII that is now a hidden public garden.
* **Late Afternoon:** Skip paid views at The Shard/Eye. Visit **Horizon 22** or **Lookout at 8 Bishopsgate** (London’s highest free viewing platforms; book free tickets online in advance).
* **Evening:** Cross **Tower Bridge** on foot and walk along the Bermondsey Wall to see the city lights reflected over the Thames.

#### Day 2: Royal Heritage & West End Hidden Alleys
* **Morning:** See the Changing of the Guard at **Buckingham Palace** (11:00 AM on select days), then walk through **St. James’s Park** to see the resident pelicans near Duck Island Cottage.
* **Afternoon:** Stroll down The Mall to Trafalgar Square and explore the **National Gallery** (Free entry). Escape the crowds by stepping into **Neal’s Yard**, a vibrant hidden alley in Covent Garden.
* **Evening:** Explore **Cecil Court** (a Victorian bookshop street) and enjoy street performers in Covent Garden.

#### Day 3: History, Eccentric Museums & Historic Pubs
* **Morning:** Visit **Sir John Soane’s Museum** in Lincoln’s Inn Fields (Free), an untouched 19th-century architect's mansion filled with antiquities and hidden doors.
* **Afternoon:** Walk through Bloomsbury to **Postman’s Park** to see the moving "Watts Memorial of Heroic Self-Sacrifice."
* **Evening:** Enjoy a drink or soft drink at **The George Inn** (London’s last remaining galleried coaching inn, frequented by Charles Dickens).

#### Day 4: World-Class Artifacts & Soho
* **Morning:** Spend hours at the **British Museum** (Rosetta Stone, Egyptian Mummies; Free entry).
* **Afternoon:** Walk through Oxford Street, Regent Street, and Carnaby Street. Grab lunch at **Seven Dials Market**.
* **Evening:** Walk through colorful Chinatown and Soho. Catch discounted theater tickets at the TKTS booth in Leicester Square if budget permits.

#### Day 5: South Kensington Museums & Hidden Palaces
* **Morning:** Head to South Kensington for the **Natural History Museum** and **Victoria and Albert (V&A) Museum** (Both Free).
* **Afternoon:** Walk to **Holland Park** and relax in the serene **Kyoto Garden** with its koi ponds and peacocks.
* **Evening:** Stroll through picturesque cobble-paved **Kensington Mews** (like Kynance Mews) and grab dinner in Queensway.

#### Day 6: Greenwich Maritime Trail
* **Morning:** Take the Uber Boat (Thames Clipper) downriver to **Greenwich** (pay using contactless card).
* **Afternoon:** Skip paid entry lines at the observatory and walk to the top of **Greenwich Park** for an iconic free panoramic view of the skyline. Walk through **Greenwich Market** for street food.
* **Evening:** Walk back across the river via the **Greenwich Foot Tunnel**—a 120-year-old Victorian pedestrian tunnel built deep beneath the riverbed.

#### Day 7: Village Vibes in Hampstead
* **Morning:** Take the Tube north to **Hampstead Village**. Walk down Flask Walk (charming historic cobblestone lane).
* **Afternoon:** Discover the **Hampstead Pergola & Hill Gardens**, a romantic, decaying Edwardian ruined terrace that feels like a secret garden (Free).
* **Evening:** Hike up **Parliament Hill** on Hampstead Heath for a sweeping sunset view of the skyline. Dine at *The Holly Bush* pub.

#### Day 8: Regent’s Canal & Alternative Culture
* **Morning:** Walk the tranquil **Regent’s Canal towpath** starting from **Little Venice** to Camden Town.
* **Afternoon:** Explore **Camden Market**. Walk up to **Primrose Hill** for high-angle views across central London parks.
* **Evening:** Visit **Shoreditch** for alternative indie venue vibes or catch live music at **Wilton’s Music Hall** (the world’s oldest surviving grand music hall).

#### Day 9: Markets & Neon Lights
* **Morning:** Stroll through **Notting Hill** and browse the **Portobello Road Market**.
* **Afternoon:** Head east to **Shoreditch** and **Brick Lane** to view street art by Banksy and Stik. Grab a quick meal at *Beigel Bake*.
* **Evening:** Visit **God’s Own Junkyard** in Walthamstow—an impressive, glowing warehouse packed with thousands of vintage neon signs (Free entry).

#### Day 10: Literary London & Departure
* **Morning:** Visit **The Wallace Collection** in Marylebone (Free)—a historic townhouse featuring fine art and armor.
* **Afternoon:** Visit *Daunt Books*, a historic Edwardian bookshop, and do last-minute souvenir shopping at Harrods or Liberty London (exterior/browsing).
* **Evening:** Take the **Elizabeth Line** (significantly cheaper than the Heathrow Express) directly to Heathrow Airport for departure.

### Food & Dining Recommendations (Budget Focused)

* **Roti King (Euston / Battersea):** Authentic Malaysian roti canai and curries (£8–£12).
* **Beigel Bake (Brick Lane):** Open 24/7. Famous hot salt beef bagel with mustard for under £6.
* **Regency Café (Pimlico):** Authentic 1950s retro café serving a classic Full English Breakfast (~£8).
* **Normah’s (Queensway):** Home-style Malaysian street food tucked inside a quiet market square.
* **The Fryer’s Delight (Holborn):** Old-school traditional Fish & Chips.
* **Supermarket Meal Deals:** Tesco, Sainsbury's, or Boots offer a sandwich, snack, and drink for £3.50–£4.50 (Essential for cutting lunch costs).
* **Mercato Mayfair:** Street food market set inside a historic converted church (Bites from £7–£10).
