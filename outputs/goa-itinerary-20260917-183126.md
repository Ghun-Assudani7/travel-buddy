### Weather & Local Customs

* **Weather (Nov–Feb Peak Season):** Dry, breezy, and pleasant, with temperatures between 20°C (68°F) at night and 31°C (88°F) during the day.
* **Embrace *Susegad*:** Goan culture values *Susegad*—a relaxed, tranquil approach to life. Take things slow and enjoy unhurried service.
* **Church & Temple Etiquette:** Dress modestly when entering active places of worship (cover shoulders and knees). Remove shoes where requested.
* **Beach Rules:** Public alcohol consumption on open beaches outside permitted beach shacks is illegal and subject to fines. Swim only in zones monitored by lifeguards (*Drishti*).

### Day-by-Day Itinerary

#### Day 1: Heritage Latin Quarter, River Forts & Speakeasy Vibe
* **Morning:** Arrive in Goa and check into your boutique stay (e.g., *Ahilya by the Sea* in Nerul or *The Postcard Velha*). Take a short private ferry across to **Divar Island** for a peaceful e-bike tour through paddy fields and Portuguese-era village streets.
* **Afternoon:** Head into **Panaji** to explore **Fontainhas** (the Latin Quarter). Stroll down *31st January Road*, pick up fresh *pastel de nata* at **Padaria Prazeres**, and sit down for an Indo-Portuguese lunch at **Horse Shoe** or contemporary coastal dining at **The Black Sheep Bistro**.
* **Late Afternoon:** Visit **Reis Magos Fort**—a restored 16th-century fortification offering panoramic, uncrowded views where the Mandovi River meets the Arabian Sea.
* **Evening:** Enjoy craft Feni cocktails at **Joseph Bar** (an iconic local watering hole in Fontainhas) followed by upscale coastal-Burmese dining at **Bomras** in Anjuna.

#### Day 2: Jungle Heritage, Hidden Coasts & Avant-Garde Goan Cuisine
* **Morning:** Drive inland to **Old Goa (Velha Goa)** to marvel at the UNESCO World Heritage **Basilica of Bom Jesus** and **Se Cathedral**. If you prefer jungle heritage, visit **Tambdi Surla**, a 12th-century basalt stone temple hidden deep within the Bhagwan Mahaveer Sanctuary.
* **Midday:** Enjoy a private home-style lunch at **Palácio do Deão** (a 213-year-old restored mansion in Quepem) serving a multi-course Goan-Portuguese meal in a lush courtyard, or opt for a tour and buffet at **Sahakari Spice Plantation**.
* **Late Afternoon:** Drive south to relax on the secluded sands of **Cola Beach** (famous for its freshwater lagoon running parallel to the sea) or **Ashwem Beach** in the north.
* **Evening:** Fine dining at **Cavatina by Avinash Martins** in Benaulim, where classic Goan dishes (like duck cabidel and seafood kalchi kodi) are reimagined into multi-course culinary art.

#### Day 3: River Mangroves, Art & Curated Feni Tasting
* **Morning:** Start early with a private guided kayak or stand-up paddleboard (SUP) tour through the tranquil mangrove channels of the **Nerul River**.
* **Midday:** Visit the **Museum of Goa (MOG)** in Saligao to explore contemporary Goan art and history. Head to **Assagao** for lunch—enjoy an authentic Goan Fish Thali at **Vinayak Family Restaurant** or coastal South Indian dishes at **Gunpowder**.
* **Late Afternoon:** Book a private tasting tour at the **Cazulo Feni Cellar** in Cansaulim to learn about cashew and coconut Feni distillation from a master distiller inside a subterranean cellar.
* **Evening:** Shop for local cashew treats at *Magsons* or souvenir azulejo tiles in Panaji before taking your private transfer to the airport for departure.

### Key Places to Visit
1. **Fontainhas (Panaji):** Historic Portuguese-style quarter lined with vibrant pastel homes.
2. **Reis Magos Fort:** Less crowded alternative to Aguada with commanding river and sea vistas.
3. **Basilica of Bom Jesus & Se Cathedral:** Iconic 16th-century architectural landmarks.
4. **Divar Island:** Serene river island best explored via e-bike or on foot.
5. **Cazulo Feni Cellar (Cansaulim):** The world’s first curated Feni tasting cellar.

### Food & Drink Recommendations
* **Must-Try Local Specialties:**
  * *Goan Fish Curry Rice:* Staple curry flavored with fresh coconut, chilis, and kokum.
  * *Pork Solantulem / Vindaloo:* Tangy, slow-cooked pork dried with kokum and spices.
  * *Crab Xec Xec:* Roasted coconut and aromatic spice crab curry.
  * *Poee:* Traditional local fermented sourdough wheat bread.
  * *Bebinca & Alebele:* Layered coconut-egg dessert and coconut-jaggery filled crepes.
* **Top Dining Spots:**
  * **Cavatina by Avinash Martins** (*Benaulim*): Reimagined luxury Goan cuisine.
  * **Padaria Prazeres** (*Panaji*): Authentic Portuguese pastries and sourdoughs.
  * **Vinayak Family Restaurant** (*Assagao*): Renowned local fish thalis.
  * **Joseph Bar** (*Panaji*): Artisanal cocktails and heritage pub atmosphere.
  * **Palácio do Deão** (*Quepem*): Fine Indo-Portuguese dining in a 200-year-old mansion.
